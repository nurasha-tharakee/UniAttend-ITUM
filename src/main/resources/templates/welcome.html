UniAttend Smart Attendance System

A high-integrity, anti-proxy university attendance platform designed specifically for the Institute of Technology, University of Moratuwa (ITUM), Diyagama, Homagama. Featuring 2-minute dynamic rolling QR tokens, campus GPS geofencing, and comprehensive medical absence tracking.

Core Engineering Capabilities

Key System Innovations

2-Minute Dynamic QR

Lecturer sessions project secure cryptographic QR tokens that expire strictly after 120 seconds with background auto-refresh to prevent photo-forwarding proxy attendance.

ITUM GPS Geofencing

Server-side Haversine geodesic validation ensures students are within the Diyagama campus perimeter (6.80894°N, 79.99282°E) when marking attendance.

Absence & Medical Module

Formal absence requests supporting 8 categories (Illness, Sports, Official Duty) with secure document uploads, lecturer reviews, and separate percentage calculations.

Institutional Security

Spring Security 6 with BCrypt encryption, CSRF protection, comprehensive system audit logging, and strict role segregation across Admin, Lecturer, and Student tiers.

Institutional Context

Institute of Technology, University of Moratuwa (ITUM)

The Institute of Technology, University of Moratuwa (ITUM) is a premier higher education institute in Sri Lanka providing high-quality engineering technology education through the National Diploma in Technology (NDT) and related technological degree programs.

Situated at its state-of-the-art green campus in Diyagama, Homagama, ITUM houses advanced engineering laboratories, auditoriums, lecture theaters, and administrative facilities. UniAttend is engineered to seamlessly integrate with ITUM academic sessions.
